Slackbot across plans

Slackbot is Slack's personal AI agent. It understands your conversations, files and connected apps, and can answer questions, draft content, build workflows and take action. How much you can use it depends on the plan. This view uses the plans you've selected on the Plans tab.

Source: slack.com/features/slackbot and slack.com/pricing. Usage limits can change; "Trial" means Free and Pro workspaces get a limited number of included Slackbot conversations. Legacy plans don't include the Slackbot agent — some AI features were available through the legacy Slack AI add-on.
